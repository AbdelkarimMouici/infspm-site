This website respects its visitors' privacy and collects as little data as possible.

## What the website does not do

- It uses no audience analytics and no advertising or tracking cookies.
- It loads no scripts or fonts from third-party sites, except the anti-bot check on the contact page (see below), and embeds no Facebook posts.
- It does not ask you to create an account.

## Contact form

When the contact form is available:

- The information you enter (name, email address, subject, message) is sent to the institute's email address and is not stored on the website. The message stays in the institute's mailbox.
- To protect against automated messages, only the contact page uses Cloudflare Turnstile. It is loaded from Cloudflare's site and processes technical data (such as IP address and browser information).

## External links

When you follow a link to another site (such as the institute's Facebook page), that site's privacy policy applies.

## Contact

For any question about this page, write to the institute's official address: infspmtebessa@inpfp.dz.
